# Brand & Logo Directions (Stage 05)

Creative identity, logo typologies, and brand design tokens for Soft Life Simulator.

---

## 1. Brand Identity Concept & Logo Typologies

### Core Concept: "The Naija Soft Life Balance"
The visual identity captures the duality of ambitious Nigerian youth culture: wanting to enjoy the good things of life ("Soft Life") while pragmatically dodging "Sapa" (extreme financial broke-ness).

### Logo Direction Options

#### Direction 1: The Soft Life Coin & Orbit (Selected Direction)
- **Mark**: A stylized circular Nigerian Naira coin token nested inside an organic, fluid "cloud" or "pill" shape, symbolizing currency protected by mindful lifestyle boundaries.
- **Wordmark**: `SOFT LIFE` set in an ultra-bold geometric sans with tight tracking, accompanied by a relaxed handwritten or monospaced script: `SIMULATOR`.
- **Tone**: Playful, high-velocity, modern fintech.

#### Direction 2: The Perforated Receipt Stamp
- **Mark**: An authentic angled receipt stamp mark reading `₦ SOFT LIFE: APPROVED`.
- **Wordmark**: Clean monospace styling reminiscent of an automated bank notification (SMS alert).

### Typography Hierarchy
- **Display Font**: `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `system-ui` with `font-weight: 800` (extra bold) for headlines.
- **Monospace Font**: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas` for transaction amounts, receipt line-items, and currency figures.
- **Body Font**: Clean sans-serif with generous line-height (`leading-relaxed`) for story readability on mobile.

## 3. Cultural Copywriting & Flavor Glossary
- **Soft Life**: Enjoying comfort, ease, and quality experiences without self-inflicted hardship.
- **Sapa**: Financial lack or temporary broke-ness.
- **Urgent 2k**: The stereotypical emergency plea from friends or acquaintances.
- **Odogwu**: An esteemed achiever or boss figure who manages resources with distinction.
- **What's the Move?**: The conversational decision prompt for each scenario.
