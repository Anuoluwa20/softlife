# Visual Reference Library (Stage 03)

Comprehensive design reference collection and UI design benchmark catalog for Soft Life Simulator.

---

## 1. Visual Archetype & Aesthetic Themes

### Archetype 1: Neo-Fintech Tactile Pop (Selected Direction)
- **Visual Language**: High-contrast dark obsidian background with electric emerald (`#10B981`) and warm mango amber (`#F59E0B`) accents. Tactile rounded cards, subtle glassmorphism borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), dynamic receipt paper textures.
- **Typography**: Bold display headline font for impact, paired with clean geometric monospace for currency numbers and transaction receipts.
- **Mood**: Fresh, youthful, credible, culturally plugged-in.

### Archetype 2: Editorial Nigerian Magazine Style
- **Visual Language**: Warm terracotta, cream parchment backgrounds, high-contrast serif headlines, postage stamp iconography.
- **Strengths**: High artistic craft.
- **Trade-off**: Can feel too nostalgic or quiet for an energetic Gen Z gaming simulator.

## 2. Component Design & Pattern Library

### 1. The Mobile HUD & Balance Indicator
- Floating or fixed top pill showing current balance with animated delta counters (`-₦3,500` floating off in red or `+₦10,000` in green).
- Day indicator badge with progress ring (Day 1 of 14).
- Soft Life Vibe Bar (0% - 100%) reflecting mental happiness / lifestyle satisfaction.

### 2. Scenario Dilemma Cards
- Card archetype: Swipeable or clickable hero card with situation imagery/icon, narrative text in large readable typography, and situational stakes.
- Action options: 2 to 3 distinct response buttons with transparent consequence badges:
  - e.g., `[Buy 20GB Data - ₦5,000 | Vibe +15]` vs `[Use Campus Wi-Fi - ₦0 | Vibe -5]`.

### 3. Digital Transaction Receipt Slip
- Perforated top and bottom edges (CSS mask/zigzag).
- Monospaced line items: Date/Day, Expense description, Category tag, Balance remaining.
- Stamp mark: "APPROVED", "SAPA DETECTED", or "FUNDS DEDUCTED".

### 4. End-of-Round Archetype Badges
- High-craft illustrated badge cards:
  - **Odogwu on Budget**: Ended with >30% funds remaining and >70% vibe score.
  - **The Weekend Millionaire**: Burned 80% of budget on days 1–4, survived on faith.
  - **The Tactician**: Perfectly balanced essentials, zero unexpected overdrafts.
  - **Sapa Survivor**: Reached Day 14 with exactly ₦250 left in account.
